Before you start

This app automates your own WhatsApp Web session. Bulk messaging is against WhatsApp's Terms of Service and your number can be banned, temporarily or permanently.

  • Only message people who expect to hear from you.
  • Keep the default delays. Faster is not better — it is a ban.
  • Start with 20–30 messages a day on a new number and build up slowly.
  • Honour opt-outs. The app records "STOP" replies automatically.
